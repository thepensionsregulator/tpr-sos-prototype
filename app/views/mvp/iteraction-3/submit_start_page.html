Important

This service is only for schemes with a valuation effective date on or after 22 September 2024.

Submit a scheme valuation

Use this service to submit valuation information for a defined benefit (DB) pension scheme.

We use the information you provide us to check that your scheme is appropriately funded and meets statutory funding requirements.

If your scheme valuation effective date is before 22 September 2024, and the scheme was in deficit on that date, use the Recovery plan service through Exchange (opens in new tab).

How to use this service

During the submission, you will need to upload the following items:

  • A copy of the statement of strategy spreadsheet including the statement of strategy contained within it. Note: You will be asked for confirmation that the statement of strategy has been signed by the chair of trustees.
  • A copy of the actuarial valuation report, signed by the scheme actuary.

If your scheme is in deficit at the effective date of the valuation, you will also need to upload:

  • A copy of the recovery plan.
  • A copy of schedule of contributions.

For schemes that are not non-segregated, non-associated, multi-employer schemes and follow a Bespoke approach, you will need to upload a summary of the trustees' analysis of the maximum affordable contributions if the scheme meets one of the following criteria:

  • It is pre relevant date.
  • It is post relevant date and does not meet the 'Bespoke low risk scheme' requirements.

For schemes that are non-segregated, non-associated, multi-employer schemes, you will need to upload a summary of the trustees' assessment of supportable risk over the reliability period if the scheme meets one of the following criteria:

  • It is a Bespoke scheme that is pre relevant date or is post relevant date and does not meet the 'Bespoke low risk scheme' requirements.
  • It is a Fast Track scheme that is not a small scheme and does not meet the 'Fast Track low risk scheme' requirements.

Make sure you have all these documents available as the submission process cannot be completed unless all of the information is submitted.

For more information about what you need to include, read our Statement of strategy guidance.
